Born Montgomery Ernest Thomas Kaluhiokalani in 1958, the Waikiki kid nicknamed Buttons for his tiny curls spun full 360s, slid tails and surfed switch-foot decades before it was normal. From a scrap of foam and a wooden paipo board at the Wall, to a Ben Aipa shortboard and the Sunkist Pro at Malibu in 1979, a long fight with addiction, and a second life teaching kids and adults with disabilities to surf, this is the Buttons Kaluhiokalani story.
