Every morning, millions of surfers check a forecast and a camera before they paddle out. The man who runs that forecast started out packing boxes in a garage in North County San Diego. This is the story of Ross Garrett: magazine kid, publisher at twenty-nine, and now CEO of Surfline, the company Sean Collins built. It covers the cameras, the critics, and the year the ocean came up into the streets.
A Surf Clips TV profile.
