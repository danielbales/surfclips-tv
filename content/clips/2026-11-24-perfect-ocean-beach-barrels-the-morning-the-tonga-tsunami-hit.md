Perfect Ocean Beach barrels in San Francisco, filmed from shore on the morning of January 15, 2022, the same morning a tsunami from the Hunga Tonga-Hunga Ha'apai eruption was crossing the Pacific.
The tsunami didn't make these waves. A winter swell did. Offshore wind and overhead sets lined up all along Ocean Beach while the eruption's tsunami was still on its way.
What happened that morning:
The Hunga Tonga-Hunga Ha'apai volcano in Tonga erupted violently on January 15, 2022 (local time), sending a tsunami across the entire Pacific Ocean and pressure waves around the Earth.
The NWS National Tsunami Warning Center (Public Tsunami Message 6, 7:28 AM PST, January 15, 2022) forecast tsunami activity at San Francisco to start at 8:10 AM PST, with a Tsunami Advisory in effect for the California coast.
NOAA tide gauges measured a maximum wave height (peak to trough) of 0.57 m (1.9 ft) at San Francisco and 1.75 m (5.7 ft) at Point Reyes (California Geological Survey, Patton et al. 2022).
The globe in this film is an approximate constant-speed travel graphic timed to the official forecast, not a tsunami model.
