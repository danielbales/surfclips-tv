A 68-year-old surfer paddled out near Surfer's Beach in Aguadilla, Puerto Rico, around noon on a Thursday. More than 24 hours passed before anyone reported him missing. When a Coast Guard helicopter crew finally found him, he was on the rocks, trying to signal the aircraft, in a spot no boat could reach and no one could walk to.
This is the story of the missing hours, the search, and the night hoist, told from the public record.
