Born in Long Beach, raised inland in Bakersfield, surfing Ventura from age ten, and the free surfer who finished tied for fourth in the world in 2010 before walking away from the tour at twenty-six. From the points of Ventura and Santa Barbara to Pipeline, a 2009 final at Lower Trestles, two perfect tens in 2012, a 2014 heat in France with Gabriel Medina, Former, the rider-owned company he co-founded in 2017, and his 2022 film Glad You Scored, this is the Dane Reynolds story.
