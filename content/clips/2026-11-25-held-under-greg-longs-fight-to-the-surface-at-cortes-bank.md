On December 21, 2012, big wave surfer Greg Long went down under a set at Cortes Bank, an underwater mountain about a hundred miles off Southern California. This is how he got back to the surface, and how his crew brought him home, told over real footage from that session and from his return to Cortes Bank in January 2014.
