A 22-year-old surfer paddled out on the Kintyre coast of Scotland on a Sunday morning. Offshore wind and tide carried him out to sea. Nobody raised the alarm until Monday lunchtime. By the second sunset, he had made peace with dying. Then a coastguard helicopter flew over.
This is the story of Matthew Bryce's 32 hours on a surfboard, told from the public record and his own words.
Survival Stories, Episode 2
HM Coastguard's advice: be prepared before you go out, tell someone where you are going, and take a way to raise the alarm. In the UK, call 999 or 112 and ask for the Coastguard.
